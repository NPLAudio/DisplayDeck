About these controls
  • These controls talk to the monitor itself over its DisplayPort or HDMI cable (a standard called DDC/CI), the same as the buttons on its front panel.
  • Only displays that are on in Windows are listed. Most monitors support this; some have a DDC/CI switch in their on-screen menu that must be on.
  • TVs usually do not listen for these commands. They are controlled with HDMI-CEC instead, which a PC cannot send without extra hardware.
  • If you switch a display to an input this PC is not on, this PC can no longer reach it. Switch it back with the display's own buttons or from the other device.
  • Some monitors number their inputs differently from the standard, so a button may select a different port than its label. The monitor's own list of inputs is used when it provides one.
