Add it to your home screen

Open DisplayDeck like an app, full screen, with its own icon.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Scan the code above with the Camera app and open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open DisplayDeck from your home screen.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome and tap the ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).

Good to know

  • The link contains a private access key. Treat it like a password and don't share the code.
  • It only works on your own network. It is not reachable from the internet; don't forward the port.
  • Settings and deleting profiles stay available on this PC only.
  • Turn phone access on or off from the tray icon: Allow other devices (phone).
